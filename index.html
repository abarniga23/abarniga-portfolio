About Me

Where creativity
meets code

I'm a second-year Computer Science student at IIT (Informatics Institute of Technology), affiliated with the University of Westminster. I've always enjoyed finding creative ways to solve problems, and that's what drew me to UI/UX design: for me, good design isn't just how something looks, but how easy and comfortable it is to use.

Alongside design, I build. My projects range from Figma prototypes and responsive websites to a full-stack task manager, a Firebase-backed health app and machine learning models in Python. I've led a four-member team, volunteered on IEEE's industry outreach team, and I'm looking for a 2027 internship where I can learn from experienced designers and developers.

How I Work

From idea to interface

01
Understand

Define who the product is for and what they need, then map the pages and user flows with a site map.

02
Wireframe

Sketch low-fidelity layouts in Figma to get structure and navigation right before any styling.

03
Design

Set a colour palette, typography and logo, then turn wireframes into high-fidelity screens for desktop and mobile.

04
Build

Bring the design to life in HTML, CSS, JavaScript or React, and connect it to a backend where needed.
